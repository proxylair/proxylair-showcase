Process · 02

From screen
to sleeve.

Every proxy is a flat, printed card — not a 3D object. Customer art goes in, a finished physical card comes out.

01

Design

Customer art is composited onto the right template for the game and card type — colors, stats, and layout matched to the real thing.

02

Print

Printed flat, at true card resolution — no shortcuts on color accuracy or sharpness.

03

Laminate

A laminate pouch gives the card the weight and finish of a real one — matte or gloss depending on the piece.

04

Cut & sleeve

Cut to exact card dimensions and sleeved, ready to shuffle into a real deck.

These are flat printed card proxies — paper, lamination, and a sleeve. Not 3D printed, no filament or resin involved.
