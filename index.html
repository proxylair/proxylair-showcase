Process · 02

From concept to
collector-grade card.

Eight steps, every one of them by hand. Customer art and ideas go in, a finished physical card comes out.

01

Consultation & Concept

Every project starts with a conversation — your concept, reference imagery, and card details, refined together before anything goes to print.

02

Image Prep & Rendering

Source imagery is inspected and calibrated by hand — brightness, contrast, saturation, and sharpness tuned for a faithful transition from screen to print. Custom artwork is composed through ProxyLair's in-house rendering engine, then held to the same standard.

03

Proof & Approval

Nothing goes to print unapproved. Every design is reviewed and confirmed before production begins.

04

Premium Printing

Printed on an Epson ET-8550 with professional-grade inks — standard cards on archival Premium Presentation Matte, foils on waterproof holographic vinyl, backs on anti-reflective semi-gloss photo stock.

05

The 24-Hour Cure

Every print rests a full 24 hours so the inks set and stabilize completely before assembly — a step most skip. We don't.

06

Assembly & Finishing

Backs are laminated and sealed. Foil fronts are mounted to premium 199gsm cardstock for real card weight and rigidity, then paired with the finished back.

07

Hand-Cutting & Inspection

Each card is trimmed and cut by hand and eye, not batch-processed — then inspected and immediately sleeved for protection.

08

Approval, Delivery & Shipping

Finished photos or video go out for your final approval before the project is carefully packaged and shipped.

These are flat printed card proxies — paper, lamination, and a sleeve. Not 3D printed, no filament or resin involved.
