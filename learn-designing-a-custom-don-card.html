Designing a Custom DON!! Card

DON!! cards are one of the more popular custom requests — bold, single-character art with real presence. Here's generally how one comes together.

What makes a DON!! card a good custom candidate

DON!! cards in the One Piece Card Game are built around a single, large piece of art rather than a lot of text or small detail — which makes them a great fit for a custom character portrait, a favorite scene, or original art. There's more room for the art to actually be the card, instead of competing with rules text for space.

Starting from a concept

It usually starts with a reference — existing art, a character, a specific scene or pose in mind — and a conversation about what the finished card should feel like. From there the art gets built or adjusted specifically for the DON!! card format, since a straight crop of an arbitrary image rarely fills that space the way a piece built for it does.

Proofing before anything prints

Same as every other card: you see a proof of the finished layout and approve it before it ever gets printed for real. For a custom piece like this, that step matters even more, since it's the last chance to adjust framing, color, or placement before it's final.

Standard or foil

A custom DON!! card can go either direction — a clean standard finish, or foil on holographic vinyl for something with more presence on the table. See Standard vs Foil Proxy Cards if you're deciding between the two.

From there, it's the same process as anything else

Print, cure for 24 hours, mount and laminate, hand die-cut, sleeve. The full breakdown is on the Process page. Get in touch with a reference or an idea and it's easy to figure out from there what a specific DON!! card would actually look like.
